Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

🔒 Your Privacy Matters: Nova is committed to protecting your privacy. All data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Nova stores the following information locally in your browser using localStorage:

  • Username: Your chosen display name
  • PIN: Hashed security PIN for panic mode (never stored in plain text)
  • Preferences: Background images, theme settings, tab cloak preferences
  • Bookmarks: Browser bookmarks you create
  • App Shortcuts: Custom application shortcuts you add

2. How We Use Your Information

All information is used solely to provide and personalize your Nova experience. This includes:

  • Displaying your chosen username and preferences
  • Protecting your session with panic mode functionality
  • Remembering your customized appearance settings
  • Maintaining your bookmarks and shortcuts

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This means:

  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • No accounts or cloud storage are used
  • Data persists only on your device and browser
  • Clearing browser data will remove all Nova settings

4. Third-Party Services

Nova integrates with and provides access to third-party services:

  • Games & Apps: Embedded content from various sources
  • Proxy Services: Scramjet and MercuryWorkshop technologies
  • External Links: Links to educational resources and services

These third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms. Nova is not responsible for their privacy practices.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Nova does not use cookies or tracking technologies. All preferences are stored in localStorage. Third-party embedded content may use their own cookies.

6. Data Security

While Nova takes reasonable precautions to secure your data, please note:

  • localStorage is accessible to anyone with physical access to your device
  • PIN protection provides basic security but is not encryption
  • You are responsible for keeping your device secure

7. Children's Privacy

Nova is designed for educational use and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. No data is transmitted externally.

8. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete all data at any time through Settings → Data → Reset All Data
  • Export or view your data using browser developer tools
  • No account deletion needed as no accounts are created

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the appropriate channels.
