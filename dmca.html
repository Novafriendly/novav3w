Copyright Policy

Nova respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users to do the same. In accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), we will respond to valid notices of alleged copyright infringement.

⚠️ Important: Nova is an aggregation platform. We do not host games, applications, or most content directly. We provide links and embed third-party content.

Third-Party Content

Nova provides access to games and applications from various sources:

  • Game Library: Sourced from GN-Math and other contributors
  • Proxy Services: Powered by Scramjet and MercuryWorkshop
  • Embedded Content: YouTube, educational platforms, and other services

If you believe content linked or embedded in Nova infringes your copyright, please follow the procedure below.

Filing a DMCA Notice

If you are a copyright owner or authorized agent and believe that content on Nova infringes your copyright, please provide a written notice containing the following information:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized agent
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed
  • Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing, with enough detail for us to locate it
  • Your contact information (address, phone number, email)
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner
  • A statement that the information in the notice is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner

Counter-Notification

If you believe content was removed in error or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notification containing:

  • Your physical or electronic signature
  • Identification of the material that was removed and its prior location
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification
  • Your name, address, and phone number
  • A statement that you consent to jurisdiction of federal court and will accept service of process from the original complainant

Repeat Infringers

Nova will, in appropriate circumstances, terminate access for users who are repeat infringers of intellectual property rights.

Limitations

Please note that:

  • Nova does not own or control most linked content
  • Removal from Nova does not affect the original source
  • We will comply with valid DMCA notices to the extent applicable
  • False DMCA claims may have legal consequences

Safe Harbor

Nova operates as a platform that links to and embeds third-party content. We make good faith efforts to respect copyright and respond to valid notices under the DMCA safe harbor provisions.

Contact for DMCA Notices

DMCA notices should be submitted through the appropriate contact channels. Please ensure your notice includes all required information listed above.
